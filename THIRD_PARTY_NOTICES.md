## Passport maps and lettering

The world map uses public-domain [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) 1:50m country geometry.

The provincial map and most city boundaries are generated from [Alibaba Cloud DataV.GeoAtlas](https://geo.datav.aliyun.com/areas_v3/bound/100000_full.json) boundary data. Source URLs and projection parameters are recorded in `src/pages/passport/journey-map/china.generated.json` and `china-cities.generated.json`.

The Taiwan county and city boundaries use the National Land Surveying and Mapping Center's 2025 [county boundary open data](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/7442), distributed under the [Open Government Data License, version 1.0](https://data.gov.tw/license). The GeoJSON was obtained from [this conversion mirror](https://geo.maderaojen.me/datasets/tw-counties/releases/v1/2026-08-07.1/data.geojson). Geometry is simplified and projected for display.

The four-character map heading uses a subset of [Ma Shan Zheng](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/mashanzheng), licensed under the SIL Open Font License. A copy of the license is at `public/fonts/MaShanZheng-OFL.txt`.
